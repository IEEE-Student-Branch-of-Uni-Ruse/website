## Contacting the branch

The [contact page](/contact) links to our community channels. When you follow those links or send a message on an external platform, that platform's privacy policy applies.

## Website appearance

The website stores your selected light or dark appearance in your browser's local storage so it can remember your preference.

## External resources

The website links to IEEE resources and third-party services. For information about IEEE's main website, see the [IEEE Privacy Policy](https://www.ieee.org/about/help/security_privacy.html).

## Questions

For questions about information published on this website, contact the branch through its [community channels](/contact).
