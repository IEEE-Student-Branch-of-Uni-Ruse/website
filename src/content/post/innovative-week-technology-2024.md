The University of Ruse, the IEEE Student Branch and the IEEE Electronics Packaging Society Student Branch Chapter announced Innovative Week of Technology in June 2024.

The programme included **Electroniad on 25 June**, the **first Balkan Summit of IEEE Student Branches and EPS Student Branch Chapters on 26 June**, and the **9th International Conference on Energy Efficiency and Agricultural Engineering on 27–29 June**.

[Read the published programme](https://eeae-conf.uni-ruse.bg/images/files/EEAE2024_IWT_Program_FINAL.pdf).
