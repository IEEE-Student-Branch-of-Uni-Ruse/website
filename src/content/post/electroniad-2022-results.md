Electroniad 2022 took place at the University of Ruse on **12 March 2022**. The published account reports participation by 24 school teams from 18 Bulgarian cities and six university teams from Bulgaria, Romania and France.

The IEEE Electronics Packaging Society Student Chapter at the University of Ruse co-organized the student section. **LongCircuit** and **L&H1**, both from the Technical University of Cluj-Napoca, took first and second place; **Atila**, represented by Atila Pandzhar from the University of Ruse, took third place.
