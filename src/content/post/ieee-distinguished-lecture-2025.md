The University of Ruse hosted an announced IEEE Distinguished Lecture by **Prof. Ricardo Reis** of the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, on **10 November 2025**. The lecture was scheduled for the Siemens Hall, with an online option.

The programme covered trends in micro- and nanoelectronic circuit design, including emerging devices, 3D circuits, flexible electronics and radiation effects. The announcement named the IEEE Bulgaria Section Joint Chapter CAS04/SSC37, the University of Ruse and its IEEE Student Branch Chapter as co-organizers.
