In September 2022, student representatives of the IEEE Electronics Packaging Society and Nanotechnology communities met in Sibiu, Romania, as part of the 9th Electronics System-Integration Technology Conference.

The meeting offered an opportunity to exchange ideas and hear about electronics and nanotechnology research. **Denis Sami** and **Assoc. Prof. Boris Evstatiev** represented the University of Ruse EPS student chapter.
