On **25 June 2024**, the international edition of Electroniad brought together 13 teams from Bulgaria and Romania. Participants built a car-parking system with an audible signal using a microcontroller and peripherals.

The first three places went to **HoloCLM**, **Byte Builders** and **Field Players**, teams from the National University of Science and Technology POLITEHNICA Bucharest. A special award for the youngest team went to **Space B&M** from Ruse. Microchip Technology in Bucharest also provided prizes.

The published account names the Electronics Packaging Society student chapter and the IEEE Student Branch at the University of Ruse among the co-organizers.
